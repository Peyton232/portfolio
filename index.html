Capital One · Senior Software Engineer

Cutting cloud costs on a high-volume transaction platform

Problem
Services processing billions of transactions were over-provisioned, and storage costs kept growing.
What I did
Right-sized EC2, tuned auto-scaling for real peak load, and added S3 lifecycle policies. I also fixed application security vulnerabilities along the way.

A service generator and a 4-month production launch

Problem
Standing up each new microservice took weeks of boilerplate, which slowed every new feature.
What I did
Built a Python service generator that scaffolds production-ready Go services, then used the same approach to ship a new "refer to underwriter" service, connected to other services over pub/sub. I also led a distributed team on testing strategy.

New services in minutes instead of weeks, and the underwriter service went from idea to production in four months.
